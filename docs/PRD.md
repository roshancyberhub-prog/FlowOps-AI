# Product requirements

FlowOps AI is a portfolio-focused operations platform combining lead management, visual workflow definitions, reliable background execution, human approvals and inspected integrations. The complete product requirements are in [REQUIREMENTS.md](REQUIREMENTS.md), with mandatory agent instructions in [CHATGPT.md](../CHATGPT.md).

The target demonstration is lead import → validation/deduplication → deterministic ICP scoring → structured AI draft → human approval → configured or explicitly simulated email delivery → inspectable execution history. Those arrows describe the target workflow, not completed features.

The foundation provides accounts, workspaces/roles, lead CRUD and audit history. Follow [IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md](../IMPLEMENTATION_STATUS.md) for actual feature and verification status. No deployment or performance target is claimed before measurement.
